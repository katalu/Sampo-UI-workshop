# Sampo-UI workshop
This material was used for the workshop on Sampo-UI, organised as a part of the study day *EU Proposal Writing: The WenDAng Initiative*.  
The event was held at the Department of Classical and Italian Philology (University of Bologna), on 09th of October 2026.  
  
Speakers: Adriana Iezzi & Katarina Lučić
